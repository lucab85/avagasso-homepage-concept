About Sarah

From engineering to empowerment.

“I started as a software engineer in the 90s, curious, creative, and determined to make things better. Two decades later, I'm still building things: teams, systems, and leaders.”

Sarah has led engineering and product teams across Europe, bringing together technical precision and human understanding. As a neurodivergent leader she treats difference as strength, and creates safe, practical mentoring for every kind of mind.
